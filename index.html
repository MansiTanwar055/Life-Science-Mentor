Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few details before you book.

What can I ask in a ₹50 session?

One focused conceptual doubt or one PYQ. For a longer explanation, choose the ₹100 / 15-minute session.

Are sessions online?

This starter site is set up for online sessions. Booking will help coordinate a suitable time and platform.

Will you guarantee NET/JRF qualification?

No. Sessions provide learning support and preparation guidance. Exam outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Do I pay before booking?

Send a request first. Confirm the time, scope and payment method directly before paying.

Can I ask about any Life Sciences topic?

Availability depends on the topic and mentor expertise. Share the topic so it can be confirmed in advance.
